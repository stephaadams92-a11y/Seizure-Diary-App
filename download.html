What it does

✓One-tap seizure and aura logging with details added later.
✓Medication records, appointments, personal diary, care plan and reports.
✓Works offline after it has been installed/opened successfully once.
✓Optional automatic encrypted cloud backup with a private recovery code.

Privacy first

Your seizure log starts on your device. Automatic cloud backup is optional. If you turn it on, the log is encrypted on your device before upload; the recovery key is kept in your recovery code.

Untold Seizure Log is a record and reminder tool. It does not diagnose seizures, detect emergencies, or replace professional medical advice or a personalised care plan.
