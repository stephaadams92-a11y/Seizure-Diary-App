First aid & care

General seizure first aid

  1. Stay with the person and keep them safe from hazards.
  2. Time the seizure. Protect the head if they are on the ground.
  3. Loosen tight clothing around the neck where practical.
  4. Do not restrain movements and do not put anything in the mouth.
  5. When convulsive movements stop, place them on their side if safe and monitor breathing.
  6. Stay until they have recovered enough to be safely left or their usual care plan says otherwise.
Emergency help: follow the person’s care plan and local emergency guidance. Seek emergency help for prolonged or repeated seizures without recovery, serious injury, breathing difficulty, or when otherwise indicated by their plan.

General information only. A personalised clinician-agreed seizure care plan takes priority.

Privacy in this free version

Seizure logs, diary entries, medications, appointments and profile details are stored locally on the device. This universal beta does not use advertising analytics. Manual backup files are created only when you ask for one. Support and contact buttons open external apps or websites.

Optional encrypted cloud backup: if you explicitly turn automatic cloud backup on, the app encrypts the complete log on your device before upload. The backup service receives encrypted ciphertext plus limited technical metadata such as app/schema version and backup time. The recovery encryption key remains in your recovery code and is not sent with the backup request. You can continue using the app without cloud backup.

Live caregiver/two-phone synchronisation is not enabled in this beta and would require a further privacy and security review before release.

Linking two phones

The shared version will use one private epilepsy profile in the cloud while each person keeps their own login.

  1. The owner chooses Add caregiver.
  2. The app creates a short-lived pairing code or QR code.
  3. The caregiver joins and the owner approves access.
  4. Both phones then receive the same seizure, aura, medication and appointment updates.
  5. Offline entries stay local and sync when internet returns.
This build stores who logged each event and is structured for shared records, but it deliberately does not claim live sync until the secure cloud backend is added.
